Saeid Zare, Ph.D.

Senior R&D Engineer · Combustion, CFD and thermal-fluid systems

I am a mechanical engineer working on combustion, heat transfer and CFD. As a Senior R&D Engineer at Faber Burner Company, I develop industrial burners from simulation and design through testing, with a focus on lower emissions. My Ph.D. at Mississippi State University was in plasma-assisted combustion, partly funded by NASA. I am based in Pennsylvania.

Research and projects

Industry · 2021 to now

Industrial burner development

Developing burner product families at Faber Burner Company, from concept through CFD simulation and combustion testing, with a focus on lower NOx emissions. Three U.S. patent filings, one granted.

Ph.D. · NASA-funded

Plasma-assisted combustion

Using nanosecond repetitively pulsed plasma discharges to stabilize inverse diffusion flames, and studying plasma-assisted ignition and discharge mode transition in a constant-volume combustion chamber.

Ph.D. · Biofuels

Laminar burning speed of biofuels

Measuring the laminar burning speed and flame structure of anisole-air mixtures, and the effect of oxygenated additives on gasoline combustion, with optical diagnostics and chemical-kinetics modeling.
