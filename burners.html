Industrial burner development

At Faber Burner Company I develop industrial burners, from concept through CFD simulation and combustion testing, with a focus on lower NOx emissions.

01

Product development

Developed two burner product families, taking each from concept through Ansys Fluent CFD simulation and combustion testing, and improved the NOx performance of the company's burners. Expanded the R&D test facilities, improved the test procedures, and built burner-sizing software that engineering and marketing use to select burners for customer applications.

02

Patented designs

Three U.S. patent filings with Faber. One premixes fuel and air through packing that acts as both mixer and flame arrestor, for lower pollution (US 2024/0167679 A1). A second covers a combustion system with an airflow eductor (application 19/237,972, filed 2025). The third, a diffuser plate, was granted as U.S. Design Patent D1,102,821 in November 2025.
